# DP-700 Card Pack

A one-page web experience that celebrates my Microsoft certification as a trading-card
booster pack. You tear the pack open, confetti bursts, and 5 holographic cards come out.
The page is shared on LinkedIn (a short screen-recorded video in the post, the live link
in the first comment), so it must look premium, run smoothly on any phone, and make a
great recording.

Owner: Juan Mallo, Data & AI Engineer, Madrid. GitHub: JuanMallodelaCalle. Portfolio: juanos.pages.dev

## Stack and repo (decided)

- Plain HTML/CSS/JS with native ES modules, **no framework and no build step**.
- `site/` is the deployable site (Cloudflare Pages output directory). `tools/` is
  dev-only (Playwright, scripts) and is never published.
- Repo: public GitHub `JuanMallodelaCalle/dp700-pack`; Cloudflare Pages project
  `dp700-pack` connected to it → https://dp700-pack.pages.dev
- Not in git: `tools/sfx/` (Pixabay files can't be redistributed standalone),
  `tools/art-src/*.png`, `video/` (renders), `node_modules/`, `tools/out/`.
- Fonts: Space Grotesk (titles, names) and IBM Plex Mono (numbers, small details),
  self-hosted. Space Grotesk is subset (`tools/fonts.mjs`; re-run after adding new
  characters). IBM Plex Mono uses IBM's official split WOFF2 files, unmodified: its OFL
  reserves the name "Plex", so never ship our own subset of it. Keep the OFL texts
  next to the fonts.

## The experience

1. **Sealed pack ("OneLake" design).**
   - Fabric teal foil wrapper (#2b9a8a → #0f4d44) with holographic topographic contour
     lines (generated by `tools/gen-topo.mjs`), over the whole pack.
   - An oversized blue shield (badge proportions, badge blue) tilted and cropped by the
     edges, with two big white stars.
   - Text: "DP-700", "Fabric Data Engineer", and a small manifest panel
     (series 2026 · contents 5 cards). No sticker/pill.
   - Real thickness (3D back and side gussets), crimped ends in the same teal, idle
     float. Hint "Tear here →". Text on the pack is not selectable.
2. **Opening.**
   - Drag across the pack (or click / Enter / Space). The strip is a chain of segments
     that curls as it peels; the pack leans slightly with the pull and eases back
     (no bounce, no wobble). Released, the strip flies off with physics and falls out of
     the bottom of the screen. The pack falls out of the bottom too. **Nothing fades
     out instead of moving**, and **no card ever leaves the frame**
     (`tools/check-frame.mjs`).
   - **Confetti:** the Fabric Forge palette (deep teal #19776E, teal #51BCAB, aqua
     #69D7C6, dark forest #0F4D44, Fabric mint #74EAC2, pale lime #A6E89A sparingly);
     strips, small squares and a few flame-like drops (our own shape).
   - **Reveal:** the stack slides out; the badge card travels to the centre, flips to
     face you with a halo and a light sweep, holds, then flies home; the four colour
     cards follow quickly. Motion is fluid and physical: no holds, no robotic beats.
3. **Final layout.**
   - **Side by side:** the badge card on the left, the 4 colour cards in a 2×2 grid in
     Microsoft logo order (red #F25022, green #7FBA00 / blue #00A4EF, yellow #FFB900).
     Deal order: badge, red, green, blue, yellow.
   - **Stacked** (badge on top, grid below) only when that gives bigger cards
     (video format and phones); a half-width desktop window stays side by side.
   - Plenty of breathing room around and between cards.
   - **Card backs:** same teal + contour lines + blue shield crest.
4. **Interaction.**
   - Cards tilt and shimmer with pointer or gyro. They must stay sharp when tilted and
     when zoomed (no `preserve-3d`, no `scale()` zoom: re-lay out the card at its size).
   - Click a card to zoom it (readable on a phone); it still tilts with holo while
     zoomed. Click again, the backdrop or Escape to put it back.
   - NO "Open another pack" button, because there are no more packs.
5. **Sound (web).** A speaker toggle in the top-right corner, muted by default, choice
   remembered. Background music: the Pixabay "retro-bgm" track, looping. Effects only
   while opening: a quiet tear that follows the drag gesture, soft confetti pop, pack
   whoosh, very quiet card landings, badge shimmer. **No sounds after the cards are
   dealt** (selecting/zooming cards is silent).
6. **`?auto=1` mode** (for recording, no cursor/UI, no sound toggle): see "Video".
7. **Accessibility.** Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Card text must be real text,
   not images. Enter or Space opens the pack and Tab moves through the cards.

## The 5 cards (content must stay accurate to the official DP-700 study guide)

**Source:** https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/dp-700

The exam has 3 domains of 30–35% each. One creature card per domain (red, green,
yellow), plus a blue card for the languages (PySpark, SQL, KQL). Do not invent numeric
stats: the only number is the real exam weight, shown as **"CU 30–35%"** (Fabric
Capacity Units in place of a life stat); the languages card says "Used across all 3
domains". No damage numbers on attacks.

Creature-card text lives in `tools/cards-src/build-cards.py` (regenerates the HTML).

### 1. Badge card — Ultra rare, full art (left)
- **Art:** `./assets/associate.svg`, full art, holo over the whole card, iridescent frame.
- **Title:** Microsoft Certified: Fabric Data Engineer Associate
- **Details:** Exam DP-700 · Juan Mallo · November 2026
- **Flavour:** "Voucher by Aleksi · Fabric Forge"
- **No score** on this or any other card.

### 2–5. Creature cards — Rare (parody layout)
Whole card in the type's colour, black text, holo only in the art window. Each has:
"STAGE 1 · Evolves from …" (joke primitive form), name, CU, type icon (our own glyph,
identical in the header and in attack costs), illustration, domain ribbon, two attacks
(real skills from the guide), weakness / resistance / retreat (coffee cups) as jokes,
flavour line, "Illus. Juan Mallo · Gen-AI", "Rare · n/5".

| 3 | green | **Pipethon** | pipe serpent | Pipeline | Cron Job | 2 · Ingest and transform data |
| 4 | blue | **Parrquet** | parrot | Code | Excel Macro | Languages · PySpark, SQL and KQL |

## Video

`tools/record.mjs` opens `?auto=1&capture=1` at 1080×1350, deviceScaleFactor 2, steps
the page's virtual clock frame by frame (fully deterministic) and exports
`video/dp700-pack.mp4` (1080×1350) and `video/dp700-pack-master.mp4` (2160×2700),
H.264 30 fps, ≤ 30 s. `--mix-only` redoes just the sound.

- **Sequence:** power-on intro (a line in the middle opening up and down, like an old
  handheld), ~1 s idle pack, tear, confetti, badge reveal, deal, light sweep, badge
  close-up (credits readable), then each colour card in close-up one by one, then an
  end card with the address (dp700-pack.pages.dev), held at the end.
- **Sound:** the page logs every cue on the virtual clock; effects are mixed at those
  times over the music (same balance as the web), −16 LUFS, AAC 192 kb/s. The music
  (170.6 BPM) is laid on its bar grid: close-ups arrive on downbeats and it ends on a
  downbeat with a final hit, **not a fade**.
- Sound effects and music: Pixabay (Pixabay Content License, no attribution required;
  credited in the README anyway).
